The Anthropic Principle

Coming back on track, even though the creatonists’s argument about the Big Bang seems a bit too far fetched, there is another, a bit more compelling argument from the creationists. This is related to the notion of Intelligent Design.

It can not be denied that the Universe has many examples of what seems to be some of the highly sophisticated and intricate designs. This argument is mainly based on the notion that “A Design implies a Designer”.

We all know the Gravitational Constant (G) It seems that it’s exact value is so crucial, that if it were slightly weaker or stronger the whole universe or at-least the stars and the planets simply could not have existed in the form they are today.

Another example is the Electromagnetic force constant ; if it were much stronger or weaker, we wouldn’t have stable chemical bonds.

If the Strong nuclear force constant were even a bit weaker, the universe would have far fewer stable chemical elements, which we believe are crucial for living organisms to exist.

These magical constants or numbers have many names like Universal Constants, Anthropic numbers, Goldilocks Constants. They have fancy names and symbols.

  • G (Gravitational Constant) ≈ 6.674 × 10^-11 m³/kg·s² Sets the strength of gravity; if stronger, stars would burn too quickly; if weaker, they wouldn't form
  • Λ (Cosmological Constant) ≈ 10^-122 Determines the expansion rate of the universe; incredibly small value that allows galaxies to form
  • mp/me (Proton-to-Electron Mass Ratio) ≈ 1836 Crucial for atomic stability and chemistry; enables complex molecular formations
  • c: universal speed of light; defines causaility in out universe
  • αs (Strong nuclear force coupling constant) Determines how strongly quarks bind together; enables stable nuclei
  • β (Beta Ratio) ≈ 1/10 Ratio of electron mass to quark masses; crucial for atomic structure
  • ε0 (Vacuum Permittivity) ≈ 8.85 × 10^-12 F/m Determines how strongly electric fields penetrate space; affects atomic structure
  • μ0 (Vacuum Permeability) ≈ 1.26 × 10^-6 H/m Related to magnetic field strength in vacuum
  • h (Planck's Constant) ≈ 6.626 × 10^-34 J·s Sets the scale of quantum effects; determines the stability of atoms
  • d (Number of Spatial Dimensions) = 3 Three spatial dimensions allow for stable orbits and complex structures
  • Ω (Total Density Parameter) ≈ 1 Describes the overall curvature of space; remarkably close to critical density
  • η (Baryon-to-Photon Ratio) ≈ 10^-9 Determines the amount of ordinary matter versus radiation in the universe

As you can see from the above, the list can be extended or debated about, but the whole point is that there exist multiple such physical constants or numbers that appear to be so meticulously fine tuned for a specific purpose of making life as we know it possible. This is said to be the work of an Intelligent Designer, the "Creator". The British physicist and cosmologist, Brandon Carter, described this concept and coined the term “Anthropic Principle”, which is a bit of a misnomer.

In theology, there is a Watchmaker analogy states that just as it is readily observed that a watch did not come to be accidentally or on its own but rather through the intentional handiwork of a skilled watchmaker, it is also readily observed that nature did not come to be accidentally or on its own but through the intentional handiwork of an intelligent designer.

In his excellent book “The Accidental Universe” ( a collection of essays ) Alan Lightman delves into this: “Why do these fundamental parameters happen to lie within the range needed for life? Does the universe care about life?”

He explains that there is no need to invoke god to explain these fine tune parameters, there is nothing divine about these Universal Constants, they are just one of many possible values and it just happens that it makes life and all the other celestial phenomena we can observe possible. At first this seems very hard to digest, a bit too big of a coincidence. But the idea of the multiverse makes it much easier to acknowledge. Just like evolution where the specific traits that were beneficial for a species survived only because those species survived and thrived due to those minute mutations that turned out to be beneficial to them. Similarly if you consider that there are zillions of universes, where all these parameters have a really wide range of values, in some cases the values are so skewed that it make it impossible to form any stars or planets, in some it makes it impossible for any complex chemical molecules to remain stable, and so on. Thereby none of them allow existence of Life. It just happens so that our universe is just a Happy Accident, nothing else.
If you are intrigued by this twilight zone where physical sciences, theology and philosophy seamlessly mingle with each other then I highly recommend this book to you.

Many scientists are still skeptical about relying on these multiple universes to explain these fine tuned parameters and are still pursuing the grand mission of physics to explain all the properties of our universe in terms of fundamental principles. But if the multiverse idea is correct, then this might just be a dream, a futile exercise for all those physicists.

In his great Sci-Fi novel, “Cosmos” Carl Sagan wrote;

Absence of Evidence is not the Evidence Of Absence.

This was clearly meant for Extraterrestrial Life Forms a.k.a. Aliens but it is also meant for God. Physicists have been trying to find explanations for these fine tuned parameters, for that one grand unified theory that explains all the properties of the universe without much luck so far.

What if the absence of evidence is indeed the evidence of absence?
